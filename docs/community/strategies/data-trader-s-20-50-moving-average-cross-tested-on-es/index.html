Our test of Data Trader's public rules for his 20/50 moving average cross, as we read them in his video BEST Moving Average Strategy: https://youtu.be/k_kSCjdf8D0
Not affiliated with or endorsed by Data Trader.
We tested the E-mini S&P (ES) alone, on hourly bars grouped into CME trade days. His video gives the rules without results to compare. The moving average type, ATR settings, risk per trade, costs and the 100,000 starting capital are our choices.

Rules

  • Daily bars.
  • Simple moving averages of 20 and 50 closes.
  • Go long when the 20 crosses above the 50.
  • Go short when the 20 crosses below the 50.
  • Enter at the next bar's open.
  • Exit with an ATR trailing stop: 3 × ATR(14).
  • Risk 1% of the balance a trade, sized on the first stop.
  • His step of first picking markets that reacted to the cross on past charts is left out.
  • Our choices: simple averages, ATR 14 × 3, 1% risk, 100,000 starting capital, a 2-tick spread and $5 commission a contract.

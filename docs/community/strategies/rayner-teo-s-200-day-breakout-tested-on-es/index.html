Our test of Rayner Teo's public rules for his 200-day breakout, as we read them in his video A Simple Trend Following Strategy (Backed By Data): https://youtu.be/ggnIGZiDoFY
Not affiliated with or endorsed by Rayner Teo.
We tested the E-mini S&P (ES) alone, on hourly bars grouped into CME trade days. His own figures are for a basket of futures, so one market neither confirms nor refutes them. Costs and the 100,000 starting capital are our choices.

Rules

  • Daily bars.
  • Go long when the close is the highest close of the last 200 days.
  • Go short when the close is the lowest close of the last 200 days.
  • Enter at the next day's open.
  • Exit with a Chandelier Exit: 6 × ATR(20) from the 20-day extreme. A close through the line exits at the next day's open.
  • Size each trade to risk 2% of the account on the 6 × ATR(20) distance.
  • One position at a time: an opposite signal while in a trade waits for the exit.
  • Our choices: 100,000 starting capital, a 2-tick spread and $5 commission a contract, trading from 22 Apr 2004 so 200 daily closes come first.

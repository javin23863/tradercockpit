How to Build a Portfolio of Trading Strategies

Send your saved strategies from Builder to Portfolio. Then let Auto build search for combinations whose strategies’ daily profits are least correlated, or set a weight for each strategy yourself in Compose; each portfolio is backtested as one account, after commission, swap and slippage.

Send strategies

Send strategies from Builder

  1. Open Builder’s Strategies tab and tick the strategies you want. Up to 100 at a time.
  2. Press Send to Auto build or Send a copy to Compose. Both send copies. Your originals stay where they were.
  3. Open Portfolio from the left sidebar. Its two tabs are Auto build and Compose.
Auto build

Let Auto build search for combinations

  1. Open Auto build, then Setup. It reads the list Simple strategies, where Send to Auto build put them.
  2. Set the size. Fewest strategies in a portfolio starts at 2, Most strategies in a portfolio at 8, and Most portfolios to keep at 100.
  3. Set the correlation limit. Most correlation between any two strategies starts at 0.3, measured on daily profit and loss in the in-sample part. Most strategies from one sector starts at 3.
  4. Choose how portfolios are ranked. Rank portfolios by net profit, return to drawdown or stagnation, in-sample or on the full sample.
  5. Choose the search method. Brute force, or Genetic search with a population size (100 to start) and a number of generations (700).
  6. Press Run. Pause and Stop are beside it. Kept portfolios go to the Kept list on the Strategies tab, with Summary, Trades, Equity, By period and On the chart views.
Compose

Set the weights yourself in Compose

  1. Open Compose. Each strategy you sent has a row: its money management, saved trades, saved net and a Weight %, 100 to start.
  2. Set Configuration. Starting capital (100,000 to start), Leverage (1) and the Data range: all shared history, or a from and to date.
  3. Press Recompute. You get the portfolio’s weights, its net profit over its trades after costs, and a log.
  4. Or press Find best weights. It searches weights on the efficient frontier. Choose the Fitness (Sharpe ratio to start, or return to drawdown, CAGR to drawdown or net profit), Max simulations (100) and the risk-free rate. A chart shows every portfolio it tried.
  5. Add a buy-and-hold leg, if you want a baseline. Add Buy and Hold, pick a market you have data for, and Add.
  6. Press Keep in Results. The portfolio is saved to Builder’s Composed portfolios list, where it opens like any result.
What can go wrong

Compose and Auto build limits

  • Fewer than two strategies: Compose needs at least two with a weight above 0%. It takes up to 50.
  • Empty lists: Auto build and Compose both say where to send strategies from when their lists are empty.
  • Recompute replaces the last portfolio. Keep the ones you want in Results first.
  • Buy and Hold left out: when its stake is less than one whole unit, the portfolio leaves it out and says so. A higher starting capital fixes it.
  • A comma in a strategy name stops it being composed. Rename it.
  • A market with no settings: when orders are sized by the portfolio’s method, a strategy whose market has no settings can’t be sized. Take it off, or turn that sizing off.
Questions

Portfolio questions

Which plan has Portfolio?

Every paid plan. It combines strategies made in Builder, which is also on the paid plans.

Can strategies on different markets go in one portfolio?

Yes. Auto build limits how many come from one sector.

Can I export a portfolio?

From the strategy list: Download, Download ticked, or Export to Excel.

Commission, Spread and Slippage in a Backtest

In Builder or a Workflows step, open Setup, then Market data: Commission, Spread and Slippage are in Costs and price detail. Use this market fills them from the market’s saved settings, so check them against what your broker charges before you run.

Steps

Set costs in Builder or a Workflows step

  1. Open Setup, then Market data. The group Costs and price detail has Slippage, Spread, Smallest stop distance and Price detail, and rows for Commission and Overnight fee (swap).
  2. Press Use this market. It is in the table Markets for this step. The status line then lists the commission, spread, slippage and swap it set.
  3. Set the commission type. Open Commission’s settings. Type: no commission, a fixed amount on each trade’s open and close, a percent of the trade’s value, a percent of each fill’s value, or an amount per lot.
  4. Set spread and slippage. Type over the values. One slippage number applies to every fill in the run.
  5. Set the swap, if you hold overnight. Charged as points or money, for long and short trades, at an hour you choose, with the day the triple fee falls on.
What gets filled in

What Use this market fills in

  • Crypto exchange markets: commission is the exchange’s taker fee as a percent of each fill, spread 0, slippage one price tick, no swap. The status line lists each change.
  • Other markets: the market’s saved settings. To set your own, open Data organization, Prepare import, and Market settings: Spread (points), Slippage (points), Commission per lot, Swap long and Swap short, then Save market settings.
  • A fee bigger than a typical bar: the app says how many times larger the round trip is than a typical bar’s range.
Worse costs

Test a strategy against worse costs

  1. Open the Stress tests step. Turn on Monte Carlo retest and press Edit.
  2. Vary the spread and the slippage. Each takes a From and a To: spread 1 to 5 and slippage 0 to 5 until you change them, over 10 runs.
  3. Change the overnight fee, if the strategy holds overnight. It is part of the Leave-out test.
  4. Read the result in Results. If the profit disappears just above your planned costs, the strategy has no room for worse fills.
Charts and paper

Costs in the Strategy Tester and paper accounts

  • Strategy Tester (Pine strategies on a chart): in the strategy’s settings, Properties has Commission with a type (%, cash per contract or cash per order) and Slippage in ticks. Both are 0 unless the script sets them.
  • Paper accounts: the account’s settings have Slippage % and a Commission % for each kind of market: crypto, stocks, futures, forex and CFDs. Both start at 0 and go up to 5%.
What can go wrong

Cost settings that are easy to miss

  • Zero costs pass without a warning. Some markets’ standard settings have no commission, spread or slippage. Check every field.
  • Points and ticks: market settings give spread and slippage in points; a crypto market’s slippage is one price tick.
  • Unknown exchange fee: crypto defaults are not applied, and the status line says so.
  • No commission type switched on: the run won’t start until you turn one on.
  • Exporting to Pine: the export is refused for a strategy tested with a spread or slippage. Retest it on TradingView timing first.
Questions

Cost questions

How do I see what a strategy was tested with?

In Results, Settings used lists the settings saved with the strategy beside the step’s current ones, and Copy settings to the step brings them back.

Where are point value and tick size?

They come with the market’s settings and are shown when you set the market up.

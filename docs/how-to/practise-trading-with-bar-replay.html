Bar Replay: Practise on Historical Data

Press Replay on a chart, click the bar you want to start from, and everything after it disappears. Press Play or step one bar at a time, and place paper orders as it goes: they fill only on bars you have already seen.

ChartsPaper tradingSaved bars
What you need

What you need before you start

  • Trader Cockpit on Windows, signed in. Replay orders go to a paper account, not a broker.
  • A chart with history: a crypto market, a stock, future or forex pair with saved bars, or a file you imported. Live charts replay too; the live feed pauses while replay is on.
Steps

Start a replay and trade it

  1. Open the chart and find the day. Scroll back to where you want to start and rest the crosshair on that bar.
  2. Press Replay in the top bar. Or press R. Replay starts at the bar under the crosshair. To start somewhere else, click any bar on the plot.
  3. Play it. The bar at the bottom has Previous, Play, Next and Speed: 0.5, 1, 2 or 4 bars a second. Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys step one bar, Home and End jump to the first and last bar.
  4. Open a paper account. Press Trade, then the Paper Trading tile. The ticket says its orders fill on the chart’s saved bars, up to the replay bar.
  5. Place an order. Buy or Sell; market, limit, stop or stop limit; a take profit and a stop loss if you want them. Confirm. The order is never sent to a broker.
  6. Step forward. A working order is checked on each new bar. The account panel shows REPLAY, with balance, equity and realised and unrealised P&L at the replay bar’s close.
  7. Leave, and come back later. Press Replay again, R or Esc to leave. Open Replay on the same chart at the same bar or a later one and the practice carries on. Orders are checked against the bars in between.
Fill rules

How replay orders fill

  • Market: at the replay bar’s close, plus the account’s slippage.
  • Limit: only when a later bar trades through the price. A touch does not fill.
  • Stop: triggers on the bar’s high or low and fills at the stop or the open, whichever is worse, plus slippage.
  • Stop limit: the limit is checked from the bar after the trigger.
  • Take profit and stop loss in the same bar: the price path inside the bar decides which came first, the same way the backtester decides.
  • The paper account’s commission and slippage apply. Orders stay working until you cancel them.
What can go wrong

Replay limits and restarts

  • Stepping back restarts the practice. Positions, orders and balance start again from that bar, and the app says so.
  • Orders fill on finished bars. On a live chart’s newest bar, step back one bar or wait for it to close.
  • Unsized markets. A file that doesn’t say whether it is futures, forex, crypto or stocks can’t size orders. A future also needs its point value, set in Data organization.
  • Options are not covered yet: their prices are premiums.
  • Broker accounts can’t trade during replay. Leave replay first.
  • Go to date is off during replay, and one practice holds at most 100 working orders.
  • The 20 most recent practices are kept. Older ones are dropped.
Practice habits

Habits that keep replay practice useful

  • Pick days at random, not days you remember.
  • Slow the speed down around your entries and exits.
  • Keep the paper balance and size at what you would really trade.
  • Write down why you took each trade before you step forward.
Questions

Bar Replay questions

Can an order see bars I haven’t reached?

No. Orders read only bars at or before the replay bar. Built-in indicators are worked out again on the bars you can see, and layers that can’t be are held back until you leave replay.

Does replay practice touch my live paper account?

No. It is kept apart from live paper cash: one practice per paper account and chart.

Which markets can I trade in replay?

Crypto, stocks, ETFs, futures, forex and CFDs, on saved history.

Is the replay bar saved with my chart?

Yes. A saved chart view keeps the replay bar and the speed.

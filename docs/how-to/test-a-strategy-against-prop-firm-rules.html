How to Test a Strategy Against Prop Firm Rules

Open a futures strategy in Results, choose Prop-firm challenge, pick a plan and check its rules. The view replays the strategy’s trades against the profit target, loss limits and days allowed, and gives a pass chance.

ResultsUS futuresPaid plans
What you need

A futures strategy with its trades

  • A backtested strategy on a US futures market (CME, CBOT, NYMEX or COMEX), open in Results. The challenge plans in the list are futures plans.
  • Costs in the backtest.
  • Your firm’s current rules, from the firm’s own site.
Steps

Pick the plan, check the rules, read the odds

  1. Open the strategy in Results and choose Prop-firm challenge. It replays the strategy’s trades against a challenge’s rules.
  2. Pick the plan in Challenge. Plans are listed by firm and account size.
  3. Open Edit rules and compare with your firm. Account, Profit target, Maximum loss and how it moves (trails end of day, trails live, or fixed), Daily loss limit, Best day at most % of profit, Minimum trading days, Calendar days to pass and Most contracts open. Change anything that differs. The plan then reads Custom, and Back to the firm’s rules undoes it.
  4. Set the trade size. Size × scales the strategy’s position, and the view shows the pass chance at each size.
  5. Tick “Count open P/L inside each trade”. This counts how far each trade went against you while it was open, so a loss limit hit by a trade that later closed fine still counts.
  6. Read the tiles. Pass chance, with its range, and typical days to pass. Then how it failed when it failed: Hit the maximum loss, Hit the daily loss limit, or Ran out of time. And Longest stretch without a new high.
  7. Look at the paths. Filter the chart to Passed or Didn’t pass and compare the two groups.
  8. Compare the plans. In “Every plan on these trades”, Compare all 16 plans runs the same trades through every plan in the list.
  9. Work out the cost. In “After you pass”, enter the Challenge price per month and the Activation fee. You get the chance to Reach a payout, Days to first payout and Paid to you per pass. “Buying challenges until one passes” adds up challenges bought, fees and net.
How it works

How the pass chance is worked out

The pass chance comes from shuffling the strategy’s trading days many times, so it is shown as a range with its confidence band. Past start dates shows how the challenge would have gone starting at different points in the strategy’s own history.

Firms change their rules, so compare the rules on screen with your account before you buy a challenge. Where a plan does not allow automated trading, the view says so.

Mistakes

Mistakes that make a pass chance look better than it is

  • Simulating a challenge on a strategy you have not tried to break. Test it first.
  • Raising the size until the pass chance looks good and ignoring how often it hits the maximum loss.
  • Forgetting the best-day rule. One big day can reach the target and still fail the challenge.
  • Counting only closed trades when the firm’s loss limit trails live.
  • Ignoring fees. If a strategy passes one challenge in four, it pays for the other three.
Questions

Questions about prop firm challenge tests

Does it tell me if I will pass?

No. It tells you how often these trades, in shuffled order, would have passed these rules.

Can I change a rule my firm has changed?

Yes. Every rule in Edit rules can be typed over.

Can I save the result?

Yes. The Export menu in Results has the prop-firm results.

What about an account I am already trading?

The Journal has prop-firm tracking for real accounts, beside your trades and notes.

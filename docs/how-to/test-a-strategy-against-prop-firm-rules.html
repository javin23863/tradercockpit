How to Test a Strategy Against Prop Firm Challenge Rules

A strategy can be profitable and still fail a challenge in its first week. The question is not whether it makes money. It is whether it reaches the target before it touches a loss limit, inside the days allowed.

ResultsUS futuresPaid plans
What you need

A futures strategy with its trades.

  • A backtested strategy on a US futures market (CME, CBOT, NYMEX or COMEX), open in Results. The challenge plans in the list are futures plans.
  • Costs in the backtest. A challenge is won or lost by small margins.
  • Your firm’s current rules, from the firm’s own site.
Steps

Pick the plan, check the rules, read the odds.

  1. Open the strategy in Results and choose Prop-firm challenge. It replays the strategy’s trades against a challenge’s rules.
  2. Pick the plan in Challenge. Plans come by firm and account size.
  3. Open Edit rules and compare with your firm. Account, Profit target, Maximum loss and how it moves (trails end of day, trails live, or fixed), Daily loss limit, Best day at most % of profit, Minimum trading days, Calendar days to pass and Most contracts open. Change anything that differs. The plan then reads Custom, and Back to the firm’s rules undoes it.
  4. Set the trade size. Size × scales the strategy’s position. The view shows the pass chance at each size: bigger reaches the target sooner and reaches the loss limit sooner.
  5. Tick “Count open P/L inside each trade”. A loss limit can be hit by a trade that later closed fine. This counts how far each trade went against you while it was open.
  6. Read the tiles. Pass chance, with its range. Typical days to pass. Then how it failed when it failed: Hit the maximum loss, Hit the daily loss limit, or Ran out of time. And Longest stretch without a new high, which is what the waiting feels like.
  7. Look at the paths. Filter the chart to Passed or Didn’t pass and see what the losing paths have in common.
  8. Compare the plans. In “Every plan on these trades”, Compare all plans runs the same trades through every plan in the list.
  9. Do the money. In “After you pass”, enter the Challenge price per month and the Activation fee. You get the chance to Reach a payout, Days to first payout and Paid to you per pass. “Buying challenges until one passes” adds it up: challenges bought, fees and net, start to finish.
What you should see

A chance, the way it fails, and what it costs.

The pass chance comes from shuffling the strategy’s trading days many times, so it is a range, shown with its confidence band. Past start dates shows how the challenge would have gone starting at different points in the strategy’s own history.

Firms change their rules. Compare the rules on screen with your account before you buy a challenge. Where a plan does not allow automated trading, the view says so.
Mistakes to avoid

The simulator is only as good as the backtest.

  • Simulating a challenge on a strategy you have not tried to break. Do that first.
  • Raising the size until the pass chance looks good and ignoring how often it hits the maximum loss.
  • Forgetting the best-day rule. One big day can pass the target and still fail the challenge.
  • Counting only closed trades when the firm’s loss limit trails live.
  • Ignoring fees. A strategy that passes one challenge in four has to pay for the other three.
Questions

Short answers.

Does it tell me if I will pass?

No. It tells you how often these trades, in shuffled order, would have passed these rules. That is the honest version of the question.

Can I change a rule my firm has changed?

Yes. Every rule in Edit rules can be typed over.

Can I save the result?

Yes. The Export menu in Results has the prop-firm results.

What about an account I am already trading?

The Journal has prop-firm tracking for real accounts, beside your trades and notes.

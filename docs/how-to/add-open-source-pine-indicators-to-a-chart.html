How to Add Open-Source Pine Indicators

Open Community, choose the Indicators tab, find one by search or sort, and press Add to chart. Nearly 1,000 open-source Pine indicators come with the app, each runs on your chart’s own bars, and its code opens as your own copy.

From Community

Add an indicator from the Community screen

  1. Open Community from the left menu. It opens on All, sorted by Newest.
  2. Choose the Indicators tab. The other tabs are Strategies, Ideas, Following and Saved.
  3. Search or sort. Type in Search the Community, or sort by Newest, Most liked, New this week, New this month or A to Z.
  4. Open a card. Each card has a picture of what the indicator draws, its category and a one-line brief.
  5. Press Add to chart. Charts opens with the indicator on it, and its code in the Pine Script panel, saved as your own copy.
  6. Press Save to keep it for later. Saved indicators are listed under Saved here and under Favorites in the chart’s indicator list.
From a chart

Add one from the chart’s Indicators dialog

  1. Press Indicators in the chart’s toolbar. The dialog is Indicators and strategies.
  2. Open Community in the left list. Or one of its groups: Moving averages, Oscillators, Levels and structure, Volatility, Candles and sessions, Trend, Statistics, Volume and Other.
  3. Or search. Search covers every tab at once.
  4. Click the row to add it. The star puts it in Favorites.
Edit the code

Read and change an indicator’s code

  1. Press Code on its row. Its code opens in the Pine Script panel as a copy.
  2. Edit it. Change inputs, colours or the logic.
  3. Press Add to chart. Your copy is saved and added. Once it is on the chart, the button reads Update on chart.
  4. Publish it, if you want. Publish sends your script for review. It goes public only after the review.
What can go wrong

Indicator limits and scripts that won’t run

  • Indicators per chart: the Free plan holds 10 on one chart, paid plans 100. Turn one off to add another; nothing saved is removed.
  • A line that can’t run: the script is not added, and the Pine Script panel lists each line as “Line N” with the reason.
  • Saved scripts: at most 200 are kept. An indicator added from Community saves a copy and counts; one added from the chart’s dialog does not.
  • Long groups: a group shows 300 rows. Search by name to find the rest.
  • No connection: what traders have published can’t be read, but the indicators that come with the app are still listed.
Questions

Community indicator questions

Do I pay for them?

No. They come with the app, and Community is on the Free plan.

Which Pine versions run?

Indicators written in Pine v4, v5 and v6.

Can I talk to other traders about one?

Signed-in traders can like an item, follow its author and comment.

How to Download Free Crypto Historical Data

In Data organization, open Connections, pick Coinbase or Kraken, choose Public spot history, enter a pair such as BTC/USD and a timeframe, and press Capture. The candles come from the exchange’s public feed, so no exchange account or key is asked for.

Steps

Capture crypto candles

  1. Open Data organization from the left menu, then Connections. Type the exchange in Search connections.
  2. Open the Coinbase or Kraken card and press Public spot history. It says free public history, no account needed.
  3. Enter the spot market. For example BTC/USD.
  4. Choose the timeframe. The list shows the exchange’s own candles and timeframes built from finer candles. For anything else, type it in Other timeframe: a count and minutes, hours, days, weeks or months, such as M45, H5 or D2. The default is H1.
  5. Choose how much history. Latest bars, or 1, 2 or 5 years where the exchange reaches that far, or the longest one read reaches. Exact UTC range takes a start and an end.
  6. Press Capture Coinbase history or Capture Kraken history. When it finishes, it says how many completed bars it saved and opens My Data on that symbol.
Use the bars

Chart, download or backtest the saved bars

  • Open Chart shows the saved bars on a chart.
  • Download saves them as a CSV file.
  • Use in Builder opens the Strategy Builder on that market, on a paid plan. Builder takes any number of minutes, hours or days.
  • From a chart, opening a Coinbase or Kraken market in the symbol box saves a copy too, and Backtest this market (paid plans) opens it in Builder.
Limits

How far back each exchange goes

  • Kraken gives only its latest 720 candles of each timeframe. Older ranges are refused, not approximated.
  • Coinbase sends 300 candles a page, and one capture reads up to 128 pages. A longer window is refused: choose a shorter one, or capture it in parts.
  • Built timeframes: when the exchange doesn’t send a timeframe, the app builds it from the exchange’s finer candles and saves both.
  • Storage: My Data holds up to 100 exchange captures. A built timeframe counts as two.
  • Spot markets only.
What can go wrong

Other things to check

  • Weekly and monthly bars in Builder: refused. Builder’s weeks start on Sunday and exchange weeks on Monday, and monthly bars aren’t taken yet. Import daily bars instead.
  • Two venues, two histories: Coinbase bars are built from Coinbase’s trades and Kraken bars from Kraken’s, so the same pair’s bars can differ between them. Test on the venue you trade.
  • Trade prints: Capture Coinbase trades or Capture Kraken trades needs both a UTC start and end, reads at most 200,000 trades in one window, and Coinbase only reads back its recent trades.
Questions

Crypto data questions

Where is the data kept?

On your computer, in the app’s data folder.

Do I need an exchange account?

No. Candles and trades come from the exchanges’ public feeds.

What are the trade prints for?

Delta and cumulative delta use them once they are saved.

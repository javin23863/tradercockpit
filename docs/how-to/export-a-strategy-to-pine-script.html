How to Export a Backtested Strategy to Pine Script

A strategy and a Pine script only agree if they trade on the same timing. Trader Cockpit retests the strategy the way a TradingView chart runs it, rules at a bar’s close and fills at the next open, and exports the Pine only from that retest.

ResultsPine Script v6Paid plans
What you need

A strategy in Results, saved with its settings.

  • A Builder strategy open in Results, on a paid plan.
  • A TradingView account to paste the script into, if that is where you want it.
Steps

Retest, then export.

  1. Open the strategy in Results. Pick the strategy you want to take with you.
  2. Open Export and choose Retest on TradingView timing. It is under Strategy code. It tests the strategy again on TradingView’s timing, with no spread or slippage, and saves that run beside the original. A full backtest runs, so give it a moment. Cancel retest stops it and adds nothing.
  3. Compare the two runs. The retest is a different run: different fill timing, no spread, no slippage. If the strategy only worked on the first timing, you want to know now.
  4. Read the Pine. When the retest finishes, its Code view opens with Pine Script v6 selected.
  5. Press Save to file. You get a .pine file.
  6. Paste it into TradingView. Open the Pine Editor there, paste the script and add it to a chart of the same symbol and timeframe.
What you should see

A script, or a reason.

The export is refused when the Pine could not match the test, and it says why: the strategy was saved without the settings it was tested with, it was tested on rules that act when a bar opens, or it was tested with a spread or slippage that Pine’s fills don’t have. The fix for all three is the retest in step 2.

Building for TradingView from the start: in Builder’s Setup, set Platform rules to TradingView timing. Everything the search keeps is then already on that timing.
Mistakes to avoid

Same rules, different bars.

  • Expecting identical trades on different data. The script matches the strategy’s rules and timing. Your TradingView chart’s bars are its own.
  • Exporting the original run’s numbers as the script’s numbers. Quote the retest.
  • Forgetting costs. The retest has no spread or slippage, so set commission and slippage in TradingView’s strategy properties to see realistic results there.
Questions

Short answers.

What else can a strategy be written as?

The Code view also writes EasyLanguage, MetaTrader and Python.

Why not export the first backtest directly?

Because it may have traded on timing or costs that a TradingView chart doesn’t use. A script that can’t reproduce its own backtest is worse than no script.

Can I test a Pine script inside Trader Cockpit instead?

Yes. Run it on your own data with the Strategy Tester.

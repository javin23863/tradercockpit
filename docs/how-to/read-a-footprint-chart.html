How to Read a Footprint Chart

A footprint chart splits each bar into price rows and shows how much traded at each price, split into selling at the bid and buying at the ask. In Trader Cockpit you turn one on for a Coinbase or Kraken market from the chart toolbar’s Order flow menu, with no exchange account.

Steps

Turn it on, then read one bar

  1. Open a Coinbase or Kraken spot market on an intraday timeframe. BTC/USD on a few minutes is a good start. No exchange account is needed: the footprint is counted from the exchange’s public trade prints.
  2. Open Order flow in the chart toolbar and choose Footprint. The same menu has Order Flow (delta and cumulative delta), Bar statistics, Trades and Trade delta.
  3. If the chart asks, press Get prints. It reads the exchange’s recent trades for the chart, which takes up to a minute. On a live chart it then keeps recording and says since when.
  4. Read the rows. Each row is a price. With Display set to Bid × Ask, the left number is what sold into the bid and the right number is what bought at the ask.
  5. Read under the bar. Δ is buying minus selling volume, and Vol is the total.
  6. Find the POC and the value area. The POC is the row with the most volume. The value area is the band of rows holding 70% of the bar’s volume, unless you change it.
  7. Find the imbalances. A row is marked when one side is at least three times the other (Imbalance %, 300 by default). Stacked levels marks several imbalanced rows in a line.
  8. Set it up your way. On the Inputs tab: Display (Bid × Ask, Ladder, Delta, Volume or Bubbles), Layout (Cluster or Profile), Row size (automatic, or Manual with Ticks per row), and what shows under each bar.
  9. Add an alert, if you want one. You can be alerted when buy imbalance is stacked.
Reading it

Three questions to ask of any bar

  • Where did the volume trade? Compare a POC at the bar’s high or low with one in the middle.
  • Who was aggressive, and did price move? Strong positive delta with no progress upward means the buying was absorbed. Strong delta that moves price means little stood in the way.
  • Where are the stacked imbalances? They mark prices where one side traded much more. Watch what happens when price comes back to them.
What it counts

What the numbers are counted from

Every number is counted from the trade prints. Prints that carry no buy or sell side are left out, and bars from before the prints begin have no cells. A Coinbase footprint is Coinbase’s trades and a Kraken footprint is Kraken’s: one venue’s view of the market.

Mistakes

Common misreadings

  • Reading one bar alone. Delta means more next to the bars around it.
  • Rows too fine to read or too coarse to show anything. Change Ticks per row until levels stand out.
  • Treating every single imbalance as a level. Stacked levels is there to pick out runs of them.
  • Forgetting where the prints begin.
Questions

Questions about footprint charts

Do I need to pay for a data feed?

No. The prints come from the exchange’s public feed.

Can I get older prints?

Within what the exchange gives back. In Data organization, open Connections, then the exchange’s card, and capture public spot trades for the window you want.

What else can I put beside it?

Market profile, volume profile, cumulative delta, and an order book heatmap that shows resting bids and offers over time.

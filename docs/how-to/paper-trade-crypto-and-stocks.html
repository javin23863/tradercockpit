How to Paper Trade Crypto and Stocks Without a Broker Account

Paper trading is only useful if it feels like the real thing: orders on the chart, fills at real prices, costs, and a record you can read afterwards. It needs no broker account and no sign-up with an exchange.

ChartsFree planSimulated money
What you need

The Free plan is enough.

  • Trader Cockpit on Windows. Charts and unlimited paper trading are on the Free plan, after a free sign-in.
  • A chart. Crypto charts carry live prices with no exchange account.
Steps

Open a chart, place an order, manage it.

  1. Open a market in Charts. Type the symbol in the symbol box and pick a timeframe. From Getting started, Open BTC/USD live does it in one click.
  2. Press Trade in the toolbar, then the Paper Trading tile. This starts a paper account. Nothing is sent to a broker.
  3. Fill in the order ticket. Buy or Sell. Order type: Market, Limit, Stop or Stop limit. Then the price, if the type needs one, and the quantity.
  4. Add your exits. Set a take profit and a stop loss. They show as lines on the chart beside the position line.
  5. Place the order. A market order fills at the live price. Each fill leaves a mark on its bar.
  6. Manage the position. Change an order with Replace. Cancel one order or Cancel all. Close position, Reverse position, or Flatten everything on this market.
  7. Read the account panel under the chart. Positions, Orders, Order history, Balance history, Activity log, Trade history and Analytics, with balance, equity and today’s P&L above them. Export CSV saves any table.
  8. Make the account realistic. A paper account has commission, leverage and shorting. In its settings you can rename it and change its slippage. Reset account changes the starting balance or currency and starts it again from empty. Add paper account gives you a second one for a different approach.
On history

Practise on saved bars with Bar Replay.

When the market is closed, or you want a day that already happened, start Bar Replay on saved bars and place paper orders as the bars play. Orders never see past the replay bar, so you can’t trade on what you could not have known.

What you should see

A trade record you can learn from.

After a week you have a list of every order and fill, a balance curve and analytics, on prices that really traded. That is enough to see whether you followed your own rules.

What paper can’t show: how you act when the money is real, and how a large order moves a thin market. Keep the size you would really trade.
Mistakes to avoid

Paper habits become live habits.

  • A paper balance ten times what you will fund. The position sizes teach you nothing.
  • No commission and no slippage on the account.
  • Resetting the account after every bad day. The bad days are the data.
  • Skipping the stop because it is only paper.
Questions

Short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. Unlimited paper trading on live prices is on the Free plan.

Which markets can I paper trade?

Any chart you can open. Orders fill on live prices where the market has a live feed, and on saved bars in Bar Replay.

Can I move to a real broker later?

Yes. On a paid plan the same Trade button lets you choose a broker to connect.

Does a paper order ever reach a broker?

No. Paper orders stay in the simulator on your PC.

How to Read an Order Book Heatmap

Open a Coinbase or Kraken chart, press Indicators, open Volume & order flow and click Order Book Heatmap. From that moment it records the exchange’s public order book and draws the resting bids and offers at each price behind the candles, brighter where more size is waiting.

ChartsOrder flowCoinbase and Kraken
Steps

Add the heatmap to a chart

  1. Open a Coinbase or Kraken pair in Charts. For example BTC/USD. No exchange account is needed: the order book is public.
  2. Press Indicators in the top bar. On the Indicators tab, open Volume & order flow, or search.
  3. Click Order Book Heatmap. Clicking the row adds it. Recording starts at once, and the chart says the heatmap fills in from now.
  4. Add the depth pane, if you want it. Click Order Book Depth & CVD in the same group: total bid and ask depth, the imbalance near the mid, delta and cumulative volume delta, in a pane under the chart.
  5. Leave the chart open. The heatmap is drawn from what it records. A line on the chart marks when recording began.
  6. Hover over a cell. The readout gives the price range, the size resting there, what traded there bought and sold, and the time in UTC.
Reading it

What the colours, dots and marks show

  • Bands of colour: resting orders at that price over time. Brighter means more size. The largest walls are named on the chart, three by default.
  • A band that ends before price reaches it: the orders were taken off the book. Large lots added and pulled are marked.
  • Dots: executed volume, green for buying and red for selling. A bigger dot holds more volume.
  • Book events: icebergs, absorption and sweeps are flagged, worked out from the recorded book. Zoom in to six hours or less to see them.
  • The right edge: the current book, the best bid and ask, and the traded volume at each price.
Settings

Settings you can change

  • Colour scale: Heat, Grey, or Bid and ask. Automatic contrast is on.
  • Depth: Resting shows each price’s own orders; Cumulative adds them up away from the mid.
  • Venues: This venue, or All venues (merged) to combine Coinbase and Kraken. Merging needs a recording of the same pair on both exchanges.
  • Dot style (3D, Gradient, Solid or Pie) and Dot shows (Total or Delta).
  • Tell me when a wall is pulled: off until you turn it on.
  • Keep recording with the chart closed: off by default. Recorded history kept: 256 MB a market by default; the oldest minutes are dropped first.
What can go wrong

What the heatmap records, and for how long

  • Nothing before you start: there is no book from before recording began, and the chart says so.
  • Recording stops when you leave unless Keep recording with the chart closed is on. Background recording covers up to 32 markets.
  • Coinbase and Kraken pairs only: other charts say their source doesn’t publish an order book here.
  • Part of the book: Coinbase is read every second and Kraken every two seconds (slower when many markets record at once), within 1% of the mid, or 5% on thin books. Orders further out are not drawn.
  • Wide windows: the longest window is 7 days, and a window with too many cells asks you to zoom in or use larger rows.
  • One venue at a time: a Coinbase heatmap is Coinbase’s book. Merge venues to see both.
Questions

Heatmap questions

Do I pay for the order book data?

No. It comes from the exchanges’ public order books.

Does it work in Bar Replay?

Yes. During replay it shows what was recorded up to the replay bar.

Can Apollo read it?

Apollo can read the order book summary: recorded walls, imbalance, buying and selling, and book events.

How to Import CSV Price Data for Backtesting

A backtest is only as good as its bars. When you import a file, every row is checked, bad rows are refused instead of repaired, and gaps stay gaps. What you test is what you brought.

DataCSV, TSV, textStays on your PC
What you need

A file of bars, and its time zone.

  • A price file: CSV, TSV or a text export, with a time, open, high, low and close on each row. Volume if you have it.
  • The time zone the file’s times are written in. This is the detail people get wrong.
  • For a TradingView chart: export the chart’s data there with the symbol and timeframe you want. That file imports as it is.
Steps

Inspect, describe, save, use.

  1. Open Data and press Import files. Choose your file.
  2. Check what it found. The columns and dates it found are shown before anything is saved.
  3. Describe the bars. Enter the symbol, the market type and the time zone of the bars. For TradeStation and MetaTrader 5 history files, pick that file format and give the bar interval as well.
  4. Fix problems before you save. A row with a bad date, a high below its low or a broken column is refused and named. Fix the file and inspect it again.
  5. Press Save private history. A private copy is kept on this computer.
  6. Press Open Chart. Look at it. The chart shows your rows as saved, with nothing resampled or filled in.
  7. Press Use in Builder. Builder also needs the instrument’s tick size and costs. Then run a backtest.
What you should see

Your bars, exactly.

The file appears under My Data and opens on a chart. Gaps in the file are kept and counted. Nothing is invented to fill them.

Check one thing by eye: find a session open or a news time you know, and see that the bar sits where it should. If it is hours off, the time zone is wrong.
Mistakes to avoid

Time is the usual culprit.

  • Guessing the time zone. Exchange time, broker server time and UTC are three different things.
  • Mixing bars stamped at their open with bars stamped at their close in one file.
  • Daily bars cut at midnight for a market whose day ends at 5 PM. For a futures or FX file with bars shorter than a day, Build daily bars in My Data makes daily bars on the exchange’s trading day.
  • Expecting missing bars to be filled. If the gap matters, get a better file.
Questions

Short answers.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. It is saved on your computer.

Where can I get data without a file?

In Data, under Connections: Coinbase and Kraken crypto history, free and with no exchange account, at any chart timeframe. Forex, metals and index history downloads inside the app at no charge. MetaTrader 5 history reads from your own terminal.

Can I run a Pine script on the imported bars?

Yes. Open the chart and add the script.

Can I get my data back out?

Yes. Saved history downloads as CSV, and any chart exports its data.

How to Import CSV Price Data for Backtesting

In Data organization, press Import files, choose your CSV, check the columns and dates it found, enter the symbol, market type and time zone, and press Save private history. The bars then open on a chart and can be used in Builder.

Data organizationCSV, TSV, textStays on your PC
What you need

A file of bars and its time zone

  • A price file: CSV, TSV or a text export, with a time, open, high, low and close on each row, and volume if you have it.
  • The time zone the file’s times are written in.
  • For a TradingView chart, export the chart’s data there with the symbol and timeframe you want. That file imports as it is.
Steps

Import, describe, save, use

  1. Open Data organization and press Import files. Choose your file.
  2. Check what it found. The columns and dates it found are shown before anything is saved.
  3. Describe the bars. Enter the symbol, the market type and the time zone. For TradeStation and MetaTrader 5 history files, pick that file format and give the bar interval too.
  4. Press Save private history. Every row is checked as it is saved, and a private copy is kept on this computer. If the file is refused, fix it and import it again.
  5. Press Open Chart. Look at the bars before you test on them.
  6. Press Use in Builder. Builder also needs the instrument’s tick size and costs. Then run a backtest.
Check it

Check the time zone by eye

The file appears under My Data and opens on a chart. Find a session open or a news time you know and see that the bar sits where it should. If it is hours off, the time zone is wrong.

Mistakes

Time zone and bar-stamp mistakes

  • Guessing the time zone. Exchange time, broker server time and UTC are three different things.
  • Mixing bars stamped at their open with bars stamped at their close in one file.
  • Daily bars cut at midnight for a market whose day ends at 5 PM. For a futures or forex file with bars shorter than a day, Build daily bars in My Data makes daily bars on the exchange’s trading day.
Questions

Questions about importing price data

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. It is saved on your computer.

Where can I get data without a file?

In Data organization, under Connections: Coinbase and Kraken crypto history, free and with no exchange account, at any chart timeframe. Forex, metals and index history downloads inside the app at no charge. MetaTrader 5 history reads from your own terminal.

Can I run a Pine script on the imported bars?

Yes. Open the chart and add the script.

Can I get my data back out?

Yes. Saved history downloads as CSV, and any chart exports its data.

Volume Profile vs TPO Market Profile

A volume profile shows how much traded at each price. A TPO profile shows how long price stayed at each price, one letter for each time period, so the two can put the point of control in different places.

ChartsProfilesValue area
Volume vs time

What each profile counts

  • Volume profile: a sideways histogram of traded volume at each price. Its POC is the price with the most volume.
  • TPO (Time Price Opportunity): one letter for every period, 30 minutes by default, in which price traded in that row. Its POC is the price visited in the most periods.
  • Value area: on both, the band around the POC that holds 70% of the profile unless you change it. VAH and VAL are its top and bottom.
  • TPO needs only the bars’ highs and lows, so it works on any intraday chart. A volume profile needs bars that carry volume.
Steps

Add both profiles to one chart

  1. Open Indicators in the chart’s top bar, then the Profiles tab. Or type the name in the search box.
  2. Add a volume profile. Session Volume Profile draws one per day by default. Periodic Volume Profile uses a period you set. Auto Anchored Volume Profile picks its own start. Visible Range Volume Profile covers what is on screen.
  3. Or mark a range yourself. Add Fixed Range Volume Profile, click the first bar, then the last. Esc cancels. Drag either edge later, or type the start and end in its settings, in UTC.
  4. Add TPO (Time Price Opportunity) from the same tab. It needs a timeframe under one day. TPO is also a chart style.
  5. Give both the same session. Both start on Each day and Full day (UTC).
  6. Set the rows. Volume: Number of Rows, 24 by default, or Ticks Per Row. TPO: Automatic, about 24 rows, or a number of price steps per row.
  7. Set the TPO letter period and initial balance. Letter period: 5, 15 or 30 minutes, 1 hour or 4 hours. Initial balance: 60 minutes by default.
  8. Turn on the lines you want. VAH, VAL and POC are on. Developing POC and Developing VA are off until you turn them on. TPO carries each POC to the right until price trades back to it.
  9. Colour the TPO rows, if you want. By the value area, time of day, volume, trades or delta. Volume, trades and delta need the market’s trade prints.
Comparing them

How to compare the two profiles

  • POCs at the same price: price spent the most time where the most volume traded.
  • POCs at different prices: heavy volume went through a price that price didn’t stay at, or price stayed at a level where little traded. Check the bars at that price to see which.
  • Rows with one or two TPO letters: price passed through those prices quickly.
  • Value areas of different widths: time spread wider than volume, or the other way round, over the same session.
  • Right-click a TPO profile to merge it with the one before or after, unmerge it, or expand it over the candles.
What can go wrong

When a profile won’t draw, or draws in part

  • No volume: the chart says these bars have none and draws no volume profile. TPO still draws.
  • Bars too long for the session: session and periodic profiles need bars shorter than the session that end inside it. Pick a shorter timeframe.
  • Estimated rows: when finer bars aren’t available, each bar’s volume is spread over its high-low range. A line under the profile says which it used.
  • Partial profiles: a profile whose start isn’t loaded is marked partial. Scroll back to load it.
  • UTC days: the default day runs midnight to midnight UTC. Set the trading hours before comparing levels from one day to the next.
  • Very tall ranges: TPO asks you to zoom in, and volume rows widen beyond 2,000 rows.
Questions

Profile questions

Can I watch the value area build during the session?

Yes. Turn on Developing POC and Developing VA.

What is the initial balance?

The price range of the first part of the session, 60 minutes by default, marked on the TPO profile.

Do I need a paid data feed?

No for TPO: it uses the chart’s bars. Colouring TPO rows by volume, trades or delta needs trade prints, which Coinbase and Kraken crypto charts read from the exchange’s public feed.

How to Paper Trade Crypto on Live Prices

Open a Coinbase or Kraken chart, press Trade, choose Paper Trading and place an order: it fills at the live price with simulated money, and no exchange or broker account is involved. Paper trading on live crypto prices is on the Free plan, after a free sign-in.

ChartsFree planSimulated money
What you need

A free sign-in and a crypto chart

  • Trader Cockpit on Windows, signed in. The Free plan includes unlimited paper trading.
  • A Coinbase or Kraken chart. Both carry live prices with no exchange account.
Steps

Place and manage a paper trade

  1. Open a crypto market in Charts. Type the symbol in the symbol box, such as BTC/USD, and pick a timeframe. From Getting started, Open BTC/USD live does it in one click.
  2. Press Trade in the toolbar, then the Paper Trading tile. This opens a paper account. Nothing is sent to a broker.
  3. Fill in the order ticket. Buy or Sell. Order type: Market, Limit, Stop or Stop limit. Then the price, if the type needs one, and the quantity.
  4. Add your exits. Set a take profit and a stop loss with the order. They show as lines on the chart.
  5. Place the order. A market order fills at the live price.
  6. Manage the position. In the account panel, Close or Reverse a position and Cancel a working order. The price ladder has Flatten, Cancel all and Reverse.
  7. Read the account panel under the chart. Positions, Orders, Order history, Balance history, Activity log, Trade history and Analytics, under the account balance, equity and P&L. Export CSV saves a table.
  8. Set the account up like yours. In the paper account’s settings: Slippage %, and for each market Commission %, Leverage 1: and whether it can Short. Reset account sets a new Starting balance and Currency and clears the orders, positions and fills. Add paper account opens a second account.
On history

Practise on saved history with Bar Replay

For a day that already happened, start Bar Replay on saved bars and place paper orders as the bars play. Orders never see past the replay bar.

US stocks

Paper trading US stocks on a paid plan

On a paid plan with your Alpaca account connected, US stocks paper trade at the live bid and ask while the market is open, from a stock’s daily chart. The ticket shows how old its price is and won’t open a position on a stale one.

Mistakes

Habits that make paper results misleading

  • A paper balance far bigger than what you would fund.
  • No commission or slippage on the account.
  • Resetting the account after a bad day. Keep the losing days in the record.
  • Skipping the stop because it is only paper.
Questions

Questions about paper trading crypto

Is it free?

Yes. Unlimited paper trading on live crypto prices is on the Free plan, after a free sign-in.

Which markets fill live?

Markets whose chart has a live bid and ask: Coinbase and Kraken crypto, and US stocks with your Alpaca account on a paid plan while the market is open. Other markets can be practised on saved history in Bar Replay.

Does a paper order ever reach a broker?

No. Paper Trading orders stay in the simulator on your PC.

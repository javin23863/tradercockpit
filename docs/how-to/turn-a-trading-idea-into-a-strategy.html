How to Turn a Trading Idea Into a Strategy

Type the idea in Apollo, with the market, the entry and the exit, and press Ask. Apollo proposes it as a Builder rule part or a checked Pine script and fills in Builder’s settings for you to review, and nothing changes until you approve it.

What you need

A paid plan, a saved market and the idea in writing

  • Trader Cockpit on a paid plan. Apollo runs on Apollo credits, which every paid plan includes.
  • A saved market to test on.
  • The idea written down: market, timeframe, what gets you in, what gets you out, and where you are wrong.
Steps

Describe it, review it, run it

  1. Open Apollo. From the left sidebar, or as a panel beside a chart.
  2. Describe the idea and press Ask. Plain words are fine. If the idea is in a chart picture, a PDF or a trade log, attach it.
  3. Read what it proposes. You get a Builder rule part, or a Pine script that has been checked before you see it. Each proposal is a card: Approve takes it, Dismiss drops it.
  4. Review the Builder settings. Apollo fills in Builder’s settings for you to review. Read every field, change what is wrong, then approve.
  5. Run the backtest. Builder runs it on your PC.
  6. Ask about the result. Apollo reads the same figures and trades you see, so you can check everything it says against the screen.
  7. Ask for a validation workflow. It recommends one for your market and timeframe. Approve the card to create it, then run the checks.
The result

What you end up with

The strategy sits in Builder with every rule visible and editable, ready to test like any other. Apollo never sends live broker orders.

Mistakes

Mistakes when working with Apollo

  • An idea with no exit. “Buy when X” is half a strategy.
  • Approving cards without reading them.
  • Taking Apollo’s summary of a result as the verdict. Open the trades.
  • Stopping at the first backtest. Run the robustness checks next.
Questions

Questions about Apollo

What does it cost to use?

Apollo credits. Each paid plan includes them, Settings shows your plan and credits for the period, and you can add more. Backtests run on your PC.

Does my idea leave my PC?

The text and attachments you send go to Apollo’s AI service so it can answer. Backtests and your data stay on your PC.

Can it use an indicator I found?

Yes. It can turn an open-source Pine indicator into a Builder building block, and checks the block against the original bar for bar on your saved bars before you save it.

Can it change my chart?

It proposes one chart change at a time as a card. You apply it yourself.

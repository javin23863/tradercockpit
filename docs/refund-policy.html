Purchases and refunds

Plans. The Free plan costs nothing. Paid plans are monthly subscriptions in US dollars, started inside the app and paid by card. A plan renews each month until you cancel.

Cancelling. Open Settings › Plan & billing in the app and choose Switch plan; your billing page opens, and you can cancel there. Your billing page shows the date your plan ends. After that the app moves to the Free plan, and your saved work stays on your PC.

Refunds. If a charge was a mistake (you were charged twice, charged after you cancelled, or couldn’t use your plan because of a fault on our side), write to support@trader-cockpit.com within 14 days of the charge and we’ll refund it. We don’t refund a month because it went unused.

Apollo credits. Credits that come with a plan are for that month and don’t carry over. A credit pack is a one-off purchase; its credits stay on your account, and a pack isn’t refunded once any of its credits are used.

Nothing here limits the rights you have under the law where you live.

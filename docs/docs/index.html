Product guide

Get to know the TraderCockpit desktop.

TraderCockpit is a Windows desktop app for strategy research. This guide explains what each workspace is for, how the app is unlocked and where your data comes from.

TraderCockpit is on a waitlist and subscriptions are not open yet. See Pricing for plans.
Workspaces

Eight workspaces, one left-hand menu.

Everything lives in a single window. The menu on the left takes you to each workspace, and every tool is available in every plan.

Getting startedYour starting point. Choose Try a sample, Connect my data or Build my first strategy. Progress follows what you actually complete in the app.
ChartsChart your own market data with studies, drawing tools, saved views, multi-chart layouts and bar replay. Open a strategy result on the chart to see its trades.
BuilderGenerate trading strategies automatically from the building blocks and rules you choose, test them on historical data and review the results. Progress, Full settings and Results keep each run and its exact settings together.
Custom projectsChain tasks such as build, retest, optimization and walk-forward into a workflow you can run again. Each task keeps its own settings.
ApolloThe research assistant built into the app. Ask it to explain a result or help set up your next test. It works through the same controls you use and asks before acting.
ModelsRun statistical and machine-learning studies on a saved copy of your data. Each result stays tied to the exact data it used.
Data organizationAdd data and find it again in one searchable library. Import price files, connect MetaTrader 5, or download Kraken and Coinbase spot market data.
SettingsYour account and product key, plan and billing, desktop updates, appearance, Apollo, help and diagnostics.
Your account

Unlock the app once with your product key.

  1. Create your TraderCockpit account on this website. You sign in to the website with Google.
  2. Choose a monthly plan. Your account then gives you a product key.
  3. Open the desktop app and enter the key on its first screen. It is stored securely on your PC, so you only enter it once.
  4. Everything in your plan opens straight away. There is no separate sign-in inside the app.

The key works while your subscription is active. If the subscription ends, the app asks for a valid key again.

Good to know

  • Google sign-in is used only on this website, never inside the desktop app.
  • Your product key is linked to your account and subscription.
  • Subscriptions are not open yet. Join the waitlist to hear when they are.
Your data

Bring your own market data.

TraderCockpit works with data you bring through your own files and accounts:

  • Files: import price history from files on your computer.
  • MetaTrader 5: connect your own MetaTrader 5 terminal.
  • Kraken and Coinbase: download spot market data.

Saved history stays on your computer. You can chart and test it offline, and it does not use Apollo credit.

Order-flow views such as footprint, delta and volume profile use the data your broker provides. If a connected source does not supply a data type, TraderCockpit tells you and suggests connecting one that does. It never fills the gap with estimated data.

More sources

More brokers and data providers are planned. They appear in the app only once they are ready to use.

Apollo

A research assistant that shows its work.

Apollo can explain a result, summarize your research or help prepare the next test. It uses the same controls you do, asks for your approval before it changes anything, and tells you before any of your material is sent off your computer.

Apollo uses the usage credit included in your plan. Higher plans include more credit, and optional top-ups of $10.00 or $25.00 add more. Apollo’s per-run limits are the same on every plan.

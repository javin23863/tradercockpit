Three steps

Install and sign in

  • Run TraderCockpit-Setup.exe. Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”, because the installer doesn’t carry a paid publisher certificate yet. Select “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
  • Open TraderCockpit. On its first screen, follow the link to get your free product key: you sign in with Google on your account page, and the key is shown there.
  • Copy the key into the app. It’s kept on your PC, so you enter it once.

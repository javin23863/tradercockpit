Are Research Lab visuals live market data?

No. Public Research Lab scenes are synthetic or illustrative unless a page explicitly states otherwise. Their purpose is to teach structure, uncertainty, and interpretation.

Open Research Lab →

Does a passing backtest or validation prove future performance?

No. Backtests, holdouts, Monte Carlo results, drawdowns, and robustness checks can narrow uncertainty under stated assumptions; they do not guarantee future outcomes.

What is the difference between Learn, Methods, How-Tos, and Docs?

Learn explains concepts, Methods explains assumptions and research procedures, How-Tos guide tasks, and Docs covers product behavior.

What does evidence provenance prove?

Provenance helps identify which source, sample, run, revision, or result a conclusion refers to. It supports auditability; it does not prove that the method or conclusion is economically valid.

What is TraderCockpit?

A Windows desktop app for trading-strategy research. It brings Builder, Charts, Custom projects, Models, Data organization and the Apollo research assistant into one window.

How much does it cost?

Core is $19.99, Trader $49.99, Quant $99.99 and Apollo Pro $150.00 per month, in US dollars. Every plan includes the same tools; higher plans run more tasks at once and include more Apollo usage credit.

How do I unlock the desktop app?

Create your account on this website (you sign in with Google) and subscribe. Your account gives you a product key. Enter it once in the desktop app and everything in your plan opens. There is no Google sign-in inside the app.

What is Apollo credit?

Apollo, the research assistant, uses usage credit. Each plan includes monthly credit, and optional $10.00 or $25.00 top-ups add more. Top-ups never renew automatically.

Which market data can I use?

Your own: price files, a MetaTrader 5 terminal, or Kraken and Coinbase spot market data. Saved history stays on your computer and works offline. Order-flow views need a source that supplies that data; the app tells you if yours does not.

Does a good backtest prove a strategy will work?

No. Backtests, holdouts, Monte Carlo results and robustness checks can narrow uncertainty under stated assumptions. They do not guarantee future results.

Are the Research Lab visuals real market data?

No. Research Lab scenes and the public examples use synthetic data. They teach structure, uncertainty and interpretation.

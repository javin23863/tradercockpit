Trader Cockpit FAQ

Short answers about the app, the plans and how to get it.

What is Trader Cockpit?

Trader Cockpit is a Windows app for building and testing trading strategies. It searches thousands of rule combinations on your market, backtests each one, then tries to break the ones it keeps. It also has charts, order flow, paper trading, broker connections, a Community, a journal and the Apollo assistant.

See every feature

Do I need to code?

No. Strategy Builder builds and backtests strategies from the market, costs and rules you set. Apollo turns an idea in plain words into a Builder rule part or a Pine script. If you write Pine, your own v5 and v6 scripts run on the chart.

Which markets and data can I use?

Crypto from Coinbase and Kraken comes with free public history and live prices, with no exchange account. Daily history for stocks, indices, forex pairs and futures downloads inside the app, and so does forex and metals history. You can also import your own price files, and use MetaTrader 5 history and quotes from your own terminal.

Market data and import

Does it run my Pine scripts?

Yes. Your own Pine v5 and v6 indicators and strategies run on the chart, and anything that can’t run is listed by line. The chart’s indicator list also has nearly 1,000 open-source community indicators, and each one opens its code as a copy.

Is there a free plan, and what is in it?

Yes, after a free sign-in and with no payment. Free has 4 charts at once and 10 indicators on each. It includes: charts with live crypto prices and no exchange account, in all seventeen chart types, with drawings; the built-in indicators, the community indicators and your own Pine indicators; order flow and profiles on crypto charts: footprint, market profile, volume profile, delta and the order book heatmap; unlimited paper trading on live crypto prices, with the full ticket, the price ladder and the account panel; bar Replay practice on saved history; watchlists, the Markets list, symbol search, and daily prices for stocks, indices, forex pairs and futures; the Community: browse, save, add to your chart, like, follow and comment; your Journal; your own price files and free history downloads; chart data and image exports; results and chart layouts you saved on a paid plan (read only). Strategy Builder and backtests, Workflows and robustness checks, Results research and retests, Portfolio, Apollo and Models are on the paid plans.

What do the paid plans cost and how do they differ?

Core is $19.99 a month, Trader is $49.99 a month, Quant is $99.99 a month and Apollo Pro is $150 a month. Every paid plan includes every feature. They differ in Apollo credits a month (4,000, 10,000, 20,000 and 30,000), Apollo tasks at a time (1, 2, 4 and 6) and Models batch size. Searches and backtests are never metered. Prices are in US dollars, billed monthly.

Compare the plans

Can I connect my broker, and does it place live orders?

31 brokers connect on a paid plan. You sign in with your broker in your own browser, and your password never goes into Trader Cockpit. With 10 of them you place orders from the chart yourself, and with the other 21 you see your positions, balances and orders. Apollo never sends live broker orders. Paper trading on live crypto prices needs no broker account.

See the broker list

Does it run on Mac?

No. Trader Cockpit is a Windows app.

Where is my data kept?

Searches and backtests run on your computer, and your strategies, results, charts and data are saved in your own folder. What you publish to the Community and what you ask Apollo are sent to our service. Your work stays on your PC, in a folder you own.

What is Apollo and what are credits?

Apollo is the assistant inside the app. Describe an idea in plain words and it turns it into a strategy you can test, and you confirm every change. Apollo runs on Apollo credits. Each paid plan includes them every month, from 4,000 on Core to 30,000 on Apollo Pro, and a credit pack adds more ($10 buys 8,000 credits, $25 buys 20,000 credits).

Apollo assistant

Can I take a strategy to another platform?

Yes. Retest a strategy on TradingView’s bar-close timing and export it as Pine. Anything that can’t match is refused, with the reason. Results also writes the strategy as EasyLanguage, MetaTrader or Python code, and trades export as CSV or Excel.

Is a backtest a promise of profit?

No. Trader Cockpit is research software and does not give financial advice. No backtest or simulation predicts future results. The guides explain what each test can and cannot tell you.

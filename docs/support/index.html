Common questions

Find the right answer quickly.

  • How do I unlock the desktop app? With the product key from your TraderCockpit account. See unlocking the app.
  • Which data sources can I use? Files, MetaTrader 5, and Kraken and Coinbase spot market data. See your data.
  • My broker does not provide order-flow data. TraderCockpit shows that the data type is missing and suggests connecting a source that has it. See your data.
  • How does Apollo credit work? Each plan includes monthly credit; top-ups add more. See Pricing.
  • I do not understand a term. Try the research glossary.
  • I cannot find a page. Use Search at the top of any page.

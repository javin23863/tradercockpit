Correlation and diversification

Understand why co-movement, clustering, and dependence matter more than simply counting how many series are in a portfolio or research set.

Plain-language definition

What is it?

Correlation and diversification: Correlation summarizes linear co-movement over a chosen sample. Diversification depends on the structure of relationships among exposures, not merely on the number of names. Correlations can change through time and can behave differently in stressed conditions.
Research question

What question does it help answer?

Which series tend to move together, and how stable is that dependence across the conditions that matter?

Bounded interpretation

What can it tell you?

  • Where exposures cluster under the chosen dependence measure and sample.
  • Which individual series have many strong relationships with others.
  • Why a collection of many positions can still behave like a small number of common risk groups.
Limitations

What can it not tell you?

  • Historical correlation is not a fixed law and can change across regimes.
  • Zero linear correlation does not imply independence.
  • A correlation matrix alone does not capture nonlinear dependence, tail dependence, liquidity, or all portfolio risks.

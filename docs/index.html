Inside Trader Cockpit

Search without code

Pick a market and set your costs. Builder builds and backtests thousands of strategies on your own PC, keeps the ones that pass your rules, and shows why the rest were dropped.

Strategy Builder

Break it before you trade it

Ready-made workflows run out-of-sample periods, other markets, slippage, Monte Carlo and walk-forward on their own. You see why each strategy dropped out.

Workflows and robustness checks

A straight verdict

Every strategy gets one grade and the reasons behind it. When it has only been tested on the data it was built on, the verdict says so.

Results

Take it with you

Your own Pine scripts run on the chart. Strategies go out the other way: retest on TradingView’s bar-close timing and export as Pine, or take the rules as MetaTrader, EasyLanguage or Python code.

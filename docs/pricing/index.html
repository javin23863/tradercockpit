Account access

A subscription should unlock the product you paid for.

  1. Stripe accepts the recurring payment.
  2. The hosted account authority verifies the billing event.
  3. The customer account receives a signed TraderCockpit entitlement.
  4. The desktop reads that entitlement through its existing account authority.
  5. Cancellation and renewal state remain synchronized.

Checkout remains unavailable until payment, account entitlement, desktop access, renewal, and cancellation all stay synchronized.

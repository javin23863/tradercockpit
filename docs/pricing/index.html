Pricing & access

Four monthly plans. One research environment.

Core, Trader, Quant and Apollo Pro are planned monthly plans. Every paid plan includes every feature; paid plans differ only in included Apollo credit and how many jobs can run at the same time. A planned Free plan covers paper trading and charts. The Windows app is in development and checkout is closed.

Account access

How access works

  1. Review the confirmed plan features and choose a monthly plan when checkout opens.
  2. Unlock the desktop app with the product key from your account page.
  3. Your desktop access follows the active subscription.
  4. Renewal and cancellation status stay tied to the same account.

The Windows app is in development. Checkout is closed; release follows readiness.

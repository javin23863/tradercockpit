Included on every plan

The same tools, whichever plan you choose.

  • Builder: generate strategies and test them on historical data.
  • Charts: chart your own market data with studies, layouts and bar replay.
  • Custom projects: reusable workflows of build, retest, optimization and walk-forward tasks.
  • Models: statistical and machine-learning studies on your saved data.
  • Apollo: the research assistant built into the app.
  • Data organization: import files, connect MetaTrader 5, or download Kraken and Coinbase spot market data.

Higher plans let more tasks run at the same time and allow larger automated workflows.

Apollo usage credit

Each plan includes monthly Apollo credit, and higher plans include more. Apollo’s per-run limits are the same on every plan.

Need more? Optional Apollo top-ups of $10.00 or $25.00 add credit. Top-ups never renew automatically.

Included credit resets each billing month. Top-up credit carries over while your subscription is active.

How it works

From subscription to unlocked desktop.

  1. Create your TraderCockpit account on this website (you sign in with Google) and choose a monthly plan.
  2. Your account gives you a product key.
  3. Enter the key once in the TraderCockpit desktop app. It is stored securely on your PC.
  4. Everything in your plan opens straight away, for as long as your subscription is active.

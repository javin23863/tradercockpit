Everything that comes with Core

Strategy Builder

  • Genetic, random or sequential search over the rule space, with progress you can watch as it runs.
  • Rule parts for conditions and price levels, built on the app's own indicator math.
  • Your own trading rules, costs and money management on each step, with a reset to default for any part.
  • Strategy templates and build recipes to start from, with a template's rules shown in plain lines before you use it.
  • Crypto markets get sensible starting exits and costs, and the app tells you what it changed.
  • Saved research ideas and candidates, and a retest of any strategy with its saved settings.
  • One button on the first screen saves the day's prices, opens Builder on BTC/USD and starts a strategy search.

Workflows and robustness checks

  • Ordered steps that run on their own: create, edit, save and reopen a workflow.
  • A library of ready-made validation workflows and strategy recipes, with search by market and timeframe.
  • Robustness checks on your results: out-of-sample periods, other markets, higher and lower timeframes, slippage and Monte Carlo.
  • Walk-forward windows, with a recompute when you change them.
  • Move a copy of a workflow to your saved market in one click. Each step keeps its own timeframe where the bars allow, and tells you when it can't.
  • Retest steps for other markets and extra held-back periods.

Results

  • One verdict for a strategy, with the reasons behind it.
  • Summary, trades, equity, results by period and by market, and trade excursions.
  • A trade list with position value, P/L %, run-up and drawdown, a column chooser and CSV.
  • Monte Carlo tests and walk-forward results on a strategy's trades.
  • Prop-firm challenge and funded-phase simulators, with rules and payouts per symbol.
  • Compare two runs side by side, and analysis tools for drawdown, edge decay and news impact.
  • Show a strategy on its chart, with where each entry and exit rule was true and why it traded or didn't.
  • The strategy written as code in EasyLanguage, MetaTrader, Pine or Python.
  • Tick strategies and send them to a retest: on other markets, on extra held-back periods, or only the ones you selected.

Portfolio

  • Auto build: build portfolios from your saved strategies, and send strategies straight from Results.
  • Compose: pick strategies yourself, with a weight for each, and see the combined results.
  • Let the app search for the best weights and see every mix it tried on a chart.
  • Add a buy-and-hold position beside your strategies.

Charts

  • Chart types: bars, candles, hollow candles, volume candles, line, line with markers, step line, area, HLC area, baseline, columns, high-low, Heikin Ashi, Renko, line break, Kagi and range.
  • One grouped indicator list: the standard built-in indicators at their usual settings, the Builder's own indicators, patterns and profiles, and your own.
  • Nearly 1,000 open-source community indicators in the same list, by category and search. Each runs on the chart and opens its code as a copy.
  • Drawings: trend line, ray, extended line, rectangle, horizontal line and ray.
  • VWAP with bands, anchored, rolling and from earnings dates; sessions, killzones and opening range breakouts in New York time; a smart-money study.
  • Machine-learning buy and sell signals with a read-out of how they would have done on the visible bars.
  • Your own Pine v5 or v6 indicators and strategies run on the chart. Anything that can't run is listed by line.
  • Ten ready-made strategies to run on any chart. The Strategy Tester shows returns, drawdowns, a buy-and-hold comparison and a sortable trade list you can download.
  • Run a strategy on the daily chart of a future, a forex pair or a US share. Futures use the contract's own tick size and point value.
  • Live US stock charts with your Alpaca account connected: today's daily candle as it forms, and 1, 5, 15 and 30 minute charts through the trading day.
  • Symbol search across markets: type a stock, future, forex pair, index or coin, including micro and mini futures by name.
  • Watchlists for any market: your own columns, grouping, flags, notes, several lists, an alert on a whole list, and hotlists that rank a whole exchange. Import, export or copy a list as text.
  • A ready-made Markets list of stocks, indices, forex pairs and futures. One click opens the daily chart with up to twenty years of history.
  • Alerts on price, values, indicator lines and drawings, with notices and sounds, and a log of what fired.
  • Side panels that drag wider or fold away: a data window and an object tree for your drawings and indicators.
  • Backtest this market: one button on a Coinbase or Kraken chart opens Builder on that market, with its fees already set.
  • Bar Replay, saved views, compare charts, and up to 16 charts at once on a paid plan.

Order flow and profiles

  • Footprint with per-bar delta and volume, value area, stacked imbalance and row size in ticks.
  • TPO market profile, with letters, colours by time, volume, trades or delta, and trading hours.
  • Volume profile: fixed range, session and periodic, with value area and point of control.
  • Measured delta, cumulative delta, trade counts, volume tails and bar statistics.
  • An order book heatmap: resting bids and offers over time under the candles, from Kraken or Coinbase, with the largest walls named.
  • Order book detail: large orders added and pulled, absorption and sweeps flagged, and a depth pane under the chart.
  • A liquidation heatmap: real liquidation prints, and an estimate of the price levels where leveraged positions would be liquidated.
  • Gamma exposure by strike, drawn from an option chain file you import.

Apollo assistant

  • Describe an idea in plain words and get a Builder rule part, or a Pine script that's checked before you see it.
  • It fills in Builder settings for you to review, and asks before it compiles or starts a run.
  • It recommends a validation workflow for your market and timeframe, and reads your strategy's results with you: the same figures and trades you see.
  • It proposes one chart change at a time, shown as a card you apply yourself.
  • It writes custom indicators for your charts, and does the maths with more than 50 built-in finance methods: position size, margin, risk of ruin, option Greeks and more.
  • Attach a trade log, a PDF or a chart picture. Your chats are saved and can be exported.
  • Turn an open-source Pine indicator into a Builder building block. Apollo checks it against the original bar for bar on your saved bars before you save it.
  • Apollo never sends live broker orders. It runs on Apollo credits, included with each paid plan.

Models

  • Studies with purged cross-validation, the deflated Sharpe ratio and triple-barrier labels.
  • Your chart indicators as features, or a strategy's own trades as the data.
  • Held-out forecasts shown on the chart, and a route from a model to a Builder strategy and a Library validation.
  • Any model you name, such as ARIMA or EGARCH, or one you describe to Apollo: fitted on the earlier bars, checked on the later bars against a baseline, and explained in plain words.
  • A sample study with clearly labelled made-up trades, so you can learn the page before using your own data.

Community

  • Browse, search and sort indicators, strategies and ideas. An indicator goes on your chart in one click; a strategy opens in the tester.
  • Tested strategies come with their rules, a results table per market and an A to F grade that says so when a result is weak.
  • Test a community strategy on your own chart, or re-run its published test on your PC and see your numbers under the published ones.
  • Every community indicator shows a picture of what it draws and one line saying what it does.
  • Editors' picks, and sorts for trending, most liked, most used and newest. Every author has a page.
  • Like an item, follow its author, comment and reply. Star an item to keep it under Saved.
  • Publish your own indicator, chart idea or tested strategy. Each is reviewed before it goes public, and you can update it later.

Journal

  • Trades, a calendar with daily notes, reports, a notebook, playbooks, routines and prop-firm tracking.
  • Import statements from many broker file formats.
  • Attach files to notes, use note templates, or draft a note with Apollo.

Market data and import

  • Coinbase and Kraken: free public history and live prices with no exchange account, at any chart timeframe; Builder takes M2 to D5.
  • Daily history for stocks, indices, forex pairs and futures, from the watchlist or the symbol box.
  • Forex, metals and index history downloaded inside the app, at no charge, with every timeframe built from it.
  • Your own price files: CSV in common layouts and TradingView's chart export, checked row by row.
  • MetaTrader 5 history and quotes from your own terminal.
  • Option chain files with open interest, one per underlying.
  • Daily bars cut on the exchange's own trading day from a futures or forex file of shorter bars, with holiday sessions handled. Futures files are matched to the exact contract (micro, mini or standard) and show its point value.
  • Saved history kept in your own data folder.

Exports

  • TradingView: retest a strategy on TradingView's bar-close timing, then export it as Pine. It refuses what can't match, with the reason.
  • MetaTrader 5: our own indicators, for use in experts.
  • Trades as CSV or Excel, charts as PNG, a printable report and prop-firm results from Results.
  • Chart data and chart images from any chart.

Paper trading

  • Our own order simulator, with commission, leverage and shorting. Orders fill at the live bid and ask on crypto charts with no broker account.
  • US stocks at the live bid and ask while the market is open, with your Alpaca account connected. The ticket shows how old its price is and won't open a position on a stale one.
  • Buy and sell on the chart, with order and position lines, take profit and stop loss, and a mark on the bar for each fill.
  • A price ladder beside the chart: click a price to place a limit or a stop, and buy or sell at market from the top.
  • Right-click the chart or the price scale to buy or sell at that price.
  • Take profit and stop loss as a price, a distance, a percent or an amount of money. Save your own exit sets per market.
  • Order lifetimes: good till cancelled, day, week, month, good till a date, immediate or cancel, fill or kill.
  • A plain warning before you confirm when an order would fill at once, when the bid and ask are wide, or when a stop is on the wrong side of the price.
  • An account panel with positions, orders, order history, balance history, activity, trades and analytics, plus CSV.
  • Practice orders on saved bars in Bar Replay, and pick the practice up where you left it.

Connect your broker

  • You sign in with your broker in your own browser, and your password never goes into Trader Cockpit.
  • Orders from the chart: Alpaca Paper, E*TRADE, Moomoo, Public.com, Stake Australia, tastytrade, Trading 212 (with paper), Wealthsimple Trade, Webull, Webull Canada. Paper trading on live crypto prices needs no broker account.
  • See your account: AJ Bell, Binance, BMO InvestorLine, Bux, Chase, CIBC Investor's Edge, Coinbase, CommSec, DEGIRO, Empower, eToro, Fidelity, Interactive Brokers (reports), Kraken, Questrade, Robinhood, TD Direct Investing, Upstox, Vanguard, Wells Fargo, Zerodha.

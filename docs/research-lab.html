Research Lab / synthetic visualizations

See the shape of a research problem.

Quantitative research is easier to reason about when uncertainty, disagreement, parameter behavior, and distributions are visible. This lab turns those ideas into interactive diagrams rather than decorative charts.

Important: every dataset on this page is synthetic and illustrative. These scenes are research education, not trading recommendations, forecasts, or claims about product capability.

10 / Multiple testing

Search more candidates and the best-looking noise gets more extreme.

This synthetic independent-null demonstration draws candidate scores from a standardized Normal distribution with no signal.

Your privacy on the TraderCockpit website

This page explains what the TraderCockpit website sends to third-party services and when those connections occur.

No cookiesWaitlist emails via KitYouTube only when you chooseUpdated October 2026
Browser storage

Optional research notes use this browser's local storage.

Ordinary browsing does not set cookies or analytics storage. The research-notes tool reads localStorage so it can restore a saved copy and writes only when you choose “Save on this device.” Those notes are not sent to a server by that tool. Clearing browser data can remove the saved copy. Normal page and asset requests still reach the site's hosting service.

Search

Site search stays on this site.

Search uses an index file served from this website. When the desktop app opens a search here, your search words stay in your browser and are not sent to a search provider.

Waitlist

Submitting the waitlist form sends it to Kit.

Kit is our email provider. The form sends your email address, your first name if you give it, and a note of where you came from (for example, a YouTube link) to app.kit.com. It never asks for payment, brokerage or trading-account details.

Video

YouTube is optional and click-to-load.

Pages do not contact YouTube when they load. Choosing “Load video from YouTube” starts a privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player. Following a link to our YouTube channel also takes you to YouTube.

Waitlist fields

Send only what the form asks for.

The waitlist asks for your email address and, optionally, your first name. Hidden fields record where you came from so we know which links work. Please do not enter passwords, brokerage logins, financial records or other sensitive information.

Third-party services

Third-party services receive data only when you use them.

Submitting the waitlist form, loading YouTube, or opening an external link sends a request to that provider. Their own privacy terms then apply.

Changes to this page

We will update this notice when the website starts sharing data with a new service.

If the site adds an account sign-in, a checkout provider, an analytics service or a similar service, this page will name it and explain what it receives.

Traceable results

Know what was tested

When you discuss a result, the useful question is exactly which data, settings and run it came from. Knowing that makes a result checkable; it does not prove the method was sound.

Synthetic research

Examples are labeled as examples

Research Lab scenes and public examples use synthetic data, and say so. They teach structure and uncertainty; they are never presented as trading results.

Product access

Availability is stated plainly

Pricing and Updates say exactly what is available, on which platform and at what price, separately from educational material.

Interpretation

Diagnostics do not become guarantees

Backtests, Monte Carlo, holdout results, drawdowns, and robustness checks can narrow uncertainty under stated assumptions. They do not guarantee future performance.

What stays attached to the evidence

Context should survive the handoff.

  • Metric definitions stay attached to sample and units.
  • Saved results stay separate from settings you change later.
  • Selection history stays relevant when interpreting a chosen result.
  • Visual summaries link back to the method or evidence they compress.
  • Examples are labeled so educational material is not mistaken for real results.

Trust / how evidence is presented

Make the evidence boundary visible.

TraderCockpit separates research education, product availability, producer evidence, and interpretation so a polished visual does not become a stronger claim than the underlying evidence supports.

Evidence identitySynthetic examples labeledNo performance promise
Provenance

Identity before interpretation

When research evidence is discussed, the useful question is which source, sample, run, revision, or other durable identity the conclusion refers to. Provenance helps support auditability; it does not prove the method was economically valid.

Synthetic research

Examples are labeled as examples

Interactive Research Lab scenes and public examples use explicitly labeled synthetic data where stated. Synthetic illustrations teach structure and uncertainty; they are not presented as trading outcomes.

Product boundary

Availability is a separate claim

Educational material can exist before a matching product capability is publicly available. Product status, platform support, and calls to action are kept separate from research explanations.

Interpretation

Diagnostics do not become guarantees

Backtests, Monte Carlo, holdout results, drawdowns, and robustness checks can narrow uncertainty under stated assumptions. They do not guarantee future performance.

What we try to preserve

Context should survive the handoff.

  • Metric definitions stay attached to sample and units.
  • Historical evidence stays distinguishable from current mutable state.
  • Selection history stays relevant when interpreting a chosen result.
  • Visual summaries link back to the method or evidence they compress.
  • Unpublished capabilities are not implied by educational content.

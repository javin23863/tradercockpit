## 2 Why

The four-link chain visible in the accepted evidence is:

`US-Iran attack pause (AP)` → `relief priced into the cash open` → `Nvidia and
XLK sold through the session` → `Nasdaq closes lower, S&P 500 finishes flat,
and VIX closes above Friday`

AP reported no US or Iranian attacks for three days and said mediators were
working toward renewed negotiations. That was enough for the S&P 500 and Nasdaq
to open above Friday while VIX opened below it.

The cash session then delivered a separate micro shock. Nvidia opened at 208.20
and closed at 196.51, only 1.07 above its 195.44 low. XLK opened at 177.83 and
closed at 174.30. AP named Nvidia and Micron among the technology drags while
most S&P 500 members gained. A concentrated technology selloff absorbed the
event relief before it could lift the broad close.

The pause itself remained fragile. AP said Hormuz was still closed and the US
naval blockade remained in force. Reuters reported no signed framework,
verification mechanism, or timeline. Those event facts explain why relief did
not become confidence; they do not supply chart levels.

## 3 Paid / hurt

- **Paid — investors holding less Nvidia concentration.** The S&P 500 was flat
  while the Nasdaq, XLK, and Nvidia all closed lower. The index difference is
  the portfolio effect of where the weight sat.
- **Paid — holders of volatility protection who stayed through the open.** VIX
  opened at 17.62, traded to 19.93, and closed at 18.67.
- **Hurt — Nvidia holders.** The stock lost 4.99% and closed in the bottom
  8.04% of its 13.31-point daily range.
- **Hurt — buyers of the relief open.** Every accepted equity chart closed below
  its own opening print.

The mechanism is concentration, not a claim that every stock sold off. A small
number of very large technology weights can pull the Nasdaq lower while a
majority of S&P 500 members rise and the broad index finishes flat.

## 6 Map

Exactly three forward scenarios, each conditional on both an event and captured
closing levels:

- **Base — pause holds, nothing is signed.** S&P 500 remains inside
  7,382.74–7,480.57, Nvidia remains inside 195.44–206.84, and VIX remains inside
  17.53–19.93. That keeps the event relief fragile and the technology damage
  contained to Monday's ranges.
- **Escalation — attacks resume or a material shipping facility is hit.** The
  market confirmation is an S&P 500 close below 7,382.74 together with a VIX
  close above 19.93. One level without its partner is incomplete confirmation.
- **De-escalation — a signed framework includes verification and Hormuz transit
  restoration.** The market confirmation is an S&P 500 close above 7,480.57
  together with Nvidia reclaiming 206.84. That pair would show event relief
  reaching both the broad index and the technology weight that blocked it.

These are conditions, not predictions.

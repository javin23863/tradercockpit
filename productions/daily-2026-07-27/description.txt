Nvidia lost 4.99% on Monday and the S&P 500 still closed green. That gap is the whole session.

The relief trade opened higher on a quiet weekend between the United States and Iran, and by the close the gap was gone. What replaced it was a rotation: money left the most crowded position in the market without leaving the market. The S&P absorbed it. The Nasdaq Composite, which carries more of the concentration, did not.

Every level in this breakdown is drawn on the chart while it is spoken. Sources: Associated Press, Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. Charts: TradingView, daily, roughly eight months.

The S&P 500 low of 7,382.74 stopped less than a point from a rising trendline projecting 7,383.41, and that is the level that decides whether this stays a rotation. Nvidia held 193.87. XLK traded through 172.41 and closed back above it. A VIX close above 19.50 would say this stopped being a rotation.

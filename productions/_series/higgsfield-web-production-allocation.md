## Meaning-first production rule

Choose the visual by what the sentence needs the viewer to understand:

1. **Mathematical claim:** show the supplied graph, the run-derived chart, or the deterministic
   diagram. Animate its real axes, points, bands, arithmetic, or labels.
2. **Concept that unfolds:** use a chalkboard/draw-on explainer or a clean motion-graphic card.
   The drawing must carry the actual relationship; an abstract squiggle is not an explanation.
3. **Trader workflow or human context:** use the existing trader-at-desk footage, TradingView
   captures, or operator-supplied photographs.
4. **Definition, comparison, or checklist:** use a concise card or split-screen diagram.
5. **Atmosphere:** allow a brief transition only when the narration is not making a claim.

Higgsfield may animate a supplied human/context image when the source content can be preserved.
It may not invent a physical object as a substitute for a chart, equation, distribution, response
surface, or Monte Carlo path. Factual graphics are animated locally in HyperFrames/Remotion so
their geometry and labels cannot drift.

Supplied screenshots are **reference material only**. They may establish which relationship needs
to be explained, but they do not appear in the cut, in review stills, or as a background plate.
Every displayed explanatory visual must be drawn in TraderCockpit's own red-on-black instrument
system and must state whether it is illustrative, derived, or recorded-run data.

- preserves the supplied source and the selected 16:9 visual treatment;

  trader-at-desk shot with a generative metaphor;

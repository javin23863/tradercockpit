## Transmission evidence

The shock class is a geopolitical supply-risk premium:

attacks on oil tankers -> higher perceived shipping and supply risk -> Brent settles above
$100 -> producer equities gap higher -> XLE and OXY surrender most of their opening gaps.

The last link broke. XLE opened 1.044 points above its prior close but finished only 0.18
points above it. OXY opened 1.14 points above its prior close but finished only 0.10 points
above it. The producer-equity response therefore did not confirm the size of the oil move.

Broad risk did confirm: the S&P 500 fell while VIX rose.

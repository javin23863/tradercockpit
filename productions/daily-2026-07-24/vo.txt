## 01 hook

Brent crude settled back below one hundred dollars a barrel Friday, according to the
Associated Press. Micron fell almost seven percent anyway.

That pair tells you more than the green S and P five hundred close. The Associated Press also
reported lower Treasury yields. The captured S and P and Dow bars both closed positive, while
the AP final recap had the Nasdaq composite lower. Apple rallied as Micron sold off.

My read is that Friday delivered relief without broad conviction. The market separated
companies instead of rewarding technology as one trade. That matters for Monday because a
flat index can hide a large difference between what is working and what is being repriced.

I want to map that separation through the completed Friday bars, then set the levels that
would show whether relief is spreading or Micron's weakness is extending.

The internal stock bars make the separation clearer.

The comparison matters. Broad exposure did not collapse when oil eased, and it did not

rotation.

## 04 apple

Apple was Friday's strongest technology counterexample.

The stock came into Friday from three hundred twenty-one dollars and sixty-six cents. It
opened at three hundred twenty-one dollars and seventy-nine cents, reached three hundred

Apple's bar prevents a lazy all-tech explanation. Money could still move into a very large
technology company while other high-value technology names fell. Friday rewarded a specific
winner rather than lifting the whole group.

For Monday, Apple has three hundred thirty-four dollars and thirty-seven cents above and
three hundred twenty-one dollars and sixty-two cents below. Those are Friday's captured high
and low.

## 06 index-math

How can Apple rise, Micron fall, and the S and P still finish almost flat?

An index is an aggregate. It combines companies with different weights and different
drivers. A strong move in one large company can offset weakness elsewhere without telling
you that the whole market is healthy. A quiet index close can therefore sit above a violent
day for a concentrated account.

Friday made that distinction visible. Apple gained three point five three percent and
finished near its high. Micron lost six point nine nine percent and finished near the lower
part of its range. The S and P gained zero point zero five percent.

Those results can coexist because the index reports the combined outcome. It does not
promise that every position experienced the same session. For Monday, the broad close and
the stock ranges must be read together.

That distinction changes the question for Monday. A green S and P close by itself cannot
repair Micron, and a Micron rebound by itself cannot prove the broad market strengthened.
Each instrument has to clear its own Friday boundary before the tape earns a broader label.

## 07 mechanism

Why did lower oil and lower yields fail to lift the whole technology complex?

Cheaper oil reduces one source of inflation pressure. Lower Treasury yields reduce one
source of valuation pressure for long-duration growth companies. Both moves helped the
macro backdrop at the margin.

Company prices still have to absorb earnings quality and capital spending. Cash generation
matters. So do competitive position and the return investors expect from AI investment. The
Associated Press reported continued concern about whether spending on AI technology would
produce profits large enough to justify elevated valuations. A Reuters session report
described a market becoming more selective around AI spending and cash burn.

Friday's bars fit that selective shape. Apple gained three point five three percent. Micron
lost six point nine nine percent. The S and P finished up zero point zero five percent.

Price alone cannot tell us which concern dominated each order. It can establish that macro
relief was insufficient to create a broad technology bid. The next closes determine whether
that separation persists.

## 08 portfolio-impact

Who got paid and who got hurt?

Dow holders finished with a positive session. S and P holders were almost unchanged. Apple
holders benefited from a strong gain that finished near the session high.

Micron holders had a different day. The stock opened below its prior close and stayed
negative into the bell. A portfolio concentrated in that name could therefore finish weak
while the broad index appeared quiet.

Oil exposure also reversed part of Thursday's surge. The Associated Press settlement at
ninety-six dollars and seventy-eight cents placed Brent back below one hundred dollars after
the prior day's move to one hundred two.

The practical lesson is exposure mapping. A broad index can move differently from a dominant
consumer-technology name, a memory-chip company, or a crude benchmark. Friday rewarded the
distinction.

A trader who watched only the broad close could miss both sides of that distribution.
Apple's gain could offset weakness elsewhere inside a diversified account, while a
hardware-heavy account could remain deeply red. The index close describes the total. It
does not describe the path each holding took to get there.

Friday's closes show where the relief reached price and where it stopped. Position labels
such as technology or growth are too broad for this tape; the individual exposures decided
the result.

Friday added to a week in which the Nasdaq underperformed. Micron finished in the weak part

The S and P's Friday bar also warns against reading a green close as a clean reset. The index
reached seven thousand four hundred sixty point nine eight and finished at seven thousand

The weekly context raises the standard for calling a repair. A single positive S and P close

the broad index cannot do that work for it.

## 10 decision-map

The base case keeps Friday's selectivity intact.

For the broad index, that means the S and P remains between seven thousand three hundred
ninety-six point five three and seven thousand four hundred sixty point nine eight. For
Micron, it means price remains between nine hundred four dollars and nine hundred sixty-seven
dollars and fourteen cents.

The broadening case needs both charts to improve together. If the S and P closes above seven
thousand four hundred sixty point nine eight and Micron closes above nine hundred sixty-seven
dollars and fourteen cents, then the relief has broader price support.

The breakdown case also needs both charts. If the S and P closes below seven thousand three
hundred ninety-six point five three and Micron closes below nine hundred four dollars, then
Friday's mixed tape has expanded into broader weakness.

A break by only one of those charts is partial evidence. The broadening or breakdown case
remains unconfirmed until both required closes occur, so a split result stays in the
selectivity branch.

Apple remains the counterexample. A close above three hundred thirty-four dollars and
thirty-seven cents extends its relative strength. A close below three hundred twenty-one
dollars and sixty-two cents removes it.

Every branch requires a close.

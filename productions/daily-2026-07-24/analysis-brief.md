# Analysis Brief — Oil Fell Below $100. Why Friday Still Wasn't Risk-On. — 2026-07-24

shock_class: macro relief with single-name selectivity

## Lead

Brent settled back below $100 and Treasury yields eased, but the relief did not produce a
broad risk-on close. The S&P 500 finished almost flat and the Dow rose while the Nasdaq
composite fell. Inside technology, Apple gained 3.53% as Micron lost 6.99%.

The useful portfolio signal is selectivity. Friday did not reward or punish technology as one
block. It separated Apple from Micron while the broad index finished near unchanged.

## 1 What moved

- AP final recap: Brent fell almost 4% to settle at $96.78 after reaching $102 Thursday.
- AP final recap: S&P 500 7,411.98, up less than 0.1%; Dow 51,947.25, up 0.5%;

- TradingView: Micron closed 920.95, down 6.99%.

## 2 Why

Lower oil and lower Treasury yields removed part of the week's macro pressure. AP and
Reuters both reported that concern about the profit payoff from heavy AI spending remained
in focus. AP identified Micron as a major reason the Nasdaq lagged during the session.

That does not establish a single cause for every close. The charts establish the result:
macro relief arrived, broad indexes diverged, and technology remained selective.

## 3 Paid / hurt

- Paid: Apple holders and Dow holders.
- Nearly unchanged: broad S&P 500 holders.
- Hurt: Micron holders and Nasdaq composite holders.
- Reversed part of the prior move: Brent exposure, based on AP's settlement.

## 4 Confirmation

- SPX versus Dow: both positive, but the Dow's 0.46% advance exceeded the S&P's 0.05%.
- Apple versus Micron: clear internal technology divergence.
- Oil relief versus Micron: diverged. Lower Brent did not coincide with a positive
  Micron close.

The divergence is the lead. A blanket "risk-on" label hides the part of Friday that mattered.

## 5 Priced in

All three major indexes ended the week lower. AP's final recap put the weekly moves at
-0.6% for the S&P 500, -0.4% for the Dow, and -2.1% for the Nasdaq composite. The Nasdaq's
larger weekly loss shows the Nasdaq underperformed across the full week.

Friday added a weak Micron close to that weekly index result. Apple's gain is the
counterexample that prevents the analysis from turning Micron's weakness into an
all-technology claim.

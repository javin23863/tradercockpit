## Dated event facts

### 1. Iran–Hormuz escalation: fresh session catalyst

- On 2026-07-22, the U.S. military announced a twelfth night of strikes against Iran. CENTCOM characterized the operation as intended to reduce threats to civilian mariners and commercial vessels and restore shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. Iran and the United States also exchanged escalatory public warnings. Source: AP, published 2026-07-22, <https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-hormuz-strait-war-july-22-2026-42ff3de8d135ad72ff3ba4d94cc0921d>.
- AP reported that the strait remained largely closed, while attacks and alerts extended across Gulf energy, shipping, and civilian-infrastructure risk. Treat this as AP's contemporaneous reporting, not a real-time vessel-flow measurement. Source: AP, published 2026-07-22, <https://apnews.com/article/iran-us-hormuz-strait-war-july-22-2026-42ff3de8d135ad72ff3ba4d94cc0921d>.
- The structural exposure is large: the EIA's latest chokepoint baseline estimated 20.9 million barrels per day of oil moved through Hormuz in the first half of 2025, roughly 20% of world petroleum-liquids consumption and one-quarter of maritime oil trade. Saudi Arabia and the UAE had about 4.7 million barrels per day of combined bypass-pipeline capacity. These are historical baselines, **not** 2026-07-22 live flows. Source: U.S. EIA, updated 2026-03-03, <https://www.eia.gov/international/analysis/special-topics/World_Oil_Transit_Chokepoints>.

### 2. Bab el-Mandeb: potential second chokepoint, with an unverified operational claim

- Yemen's Houthis declared that Saudi-linked shipping would be blocked at Bab el-Mandeb. They claimed six ships had rerouted, but AP said that claim could not be independently confirmed. Do **not** script the six-ship figure as established fact. Source: AP, published 2026-07-21 and updated 2026-07-22, <https://apnews.com/article/yemen-saudi-houthis-attack-shipping-red-sea-4e25fbdad821762e478173e6308884fb>.
- The EIA baseline estimated 4.2 million barrels per day transited Bab el-Mandeb in the first half of 2025. Its route analysis says diverting Arabian Sea–Europe traffic around the Cape of Good Hope adds about 15 days. These are structural estimates, not evidence that the route was fully closed on 2026-07-22. Source: U.S. EIA, updated 2026-03-03, <https://www.eia.gov/international/analysis/special-topics/World_Oil_Transit_Chokepoints>.

### 3. U.S. petroleum inventories: a clean divergence test

- For the week ending 2026-07-17, U.S. commercial crude inventories excluding the Strategic Petroleum Reserve rose by 2.0 million barrels to 411.7 million barrels, still 6% below the 2021–2025 seasonal average. Gasoline inventories rose 0.8 million barrels; distillates rose 1.4 million barrels; refinery utilization was 96.1%. Source: U.S. EIA, published 2026-07-22, <https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=67868>.
- This release creates a testable divergence, not a prewritten conclusion: if the local completed-session charts show oil and energy equities rising despite the inventory build, the stronger read is that geopolitical/shipping risk dominated the domestic stock signal. If the local charts do not confirm that cross-asset pattern, reject the thesis.

### 4. Brazil tariff action became effective

- USTR's Section 301 action imposed an additional 25% duty on covered Brazilian imports, with product exclusions, effective at 12:01 a.m. ET on 2026-07-22. Source: USTR Federal Register notice, published 2026-07-15, <https://ustr.gov/sites/default/files/files/Issue_Areas/Enforcement/Section%20301/Brazil%20301%20Final%20Action%20FRN%207-15-2026%20final.pdf>.
- USTR said the action followed findings concerning digital trade and electronic-payment services, preferential tariffs, anti-corruption enforcement, intellectual property, ethanol, and deforestation. This is the U.S. government's stated rationale, not an independent adjudication of those claims. Source: USTR, published 2026-07-15, <https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/july/ustr-section-301-action-brazils-unreasonable-acts-policies-and-practices>.
